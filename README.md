# Atlas-Open-Source
AI should give people more opportunities, not take them away.

I built Atlas because I believe useful technology should be available to everyone, not just those who can afford it.

It started as a free tool to help RV owners. Now, I'm opening its source code to the world.

**Technology should empower people, not leave them behind.**

**What is ROHRO Atlas?**

Atlas is an AI-powered educational assistant originally built to help RV owners understand, troubleshoot, and learn about their RVs.

It can search relevant educational resources, guide people through problems one step at a time, explain complicated topics in plain language, and help people make more informed decisions.

But Atlas is more than a question-and-answer chatbot. It has a learning and improvement system designed to evaluate its own performance, identify weaknesses, and propose ways to improve regardless of the AI model used.

It was built independently, with a focus on practical knowledge, accessibility, and keeping technology affordable.

Now, its source code is being opened to the world so others can learn from it, build on it, and adapt the technology for their own communities.

**Built for RV owners. Designed to show what's possible. Shared for everyone.**

# Important: Before You Get Started

This release of Atlas has been stripped of ROHRO-specific branding, educational content, instructions, and integrations. These have been replaced with placeholders for you to configure with your own information, knowledge, objectives, and services.

Atlas is a foundation, not a finished assistant for every application. You'll need to make it your own by supplying its identity, purpose, knowledge sources, and relevant configuration.

The goal is to give you the freedom to build an AI assistant for virtually any purpose, without being tied to one particular AI model or application.

## What Could You Build?

A few ideas:
- **Education:** A personal tutor that teaches, explains, and adapts to learners.
- **Technical Support:** An assistant that diagnoses problems and guides people through solutions.
- **Business:** An assistant that understands your operations, procedures, and services.
- **Research:** An assistant that collects information, evaluates its findings, and improves its knowledge.
- **Personal Projects:** An assistant built around your hobbies, interests, or specialized knowledge.
- **Development:** An AI that evaluates its own performance and proposes ways to improve.

These are just starting points. Your imagination is the limit.
